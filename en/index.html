About

I am Lucas Orth, an IT specialist with an M.Sc. in Information Systems (Business Informatics) from Losheim am See in Saarland, Germany. In 2019 I started my own business with web design and IT consulting for small and medium-sized companies in Saarland, Rhineland-Palatinate and Luxembourg.

Over time, what happens after the sale became my main interest. That is what Skrift is about: Skrift Kundenwerk is the consultancy, the Skrift Handschrift-Manufaktur writes the letters. I studied business informatics at ASW Berufsakademie and at Trier University. I am a member of the BNI Chapter Merzig.
